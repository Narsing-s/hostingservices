# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making Nexus Hosting an open, welcoming, technically rigorous community. Contributors, maintainers, users, and guests should be treated with respect regardless of background, experience level, identity, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and professional.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Give actionable feedback focused on code, design, reliability, security, and user impact.
- Welcome questions from newcomers.
- Protect user data, credentials, and private infrastructure details.
- Disclose security issues privately through the process in `SECURITY.md`.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or intimidation.
- Sexualized or hateful language or imagery.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting project infrastructure, CI, releases, or community spaces.
- Introducing malware, credential theft, backdoors, or intentionally unsafe code.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented when appropriate, and focused on protecting the community and project.

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through the repository's private security/contact channel rather than opening a public issue containing sensitive details.

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, documentation, community events, and project-managed communication channels.
